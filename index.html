一、本机处理的信息

为实现情景来电功能，应用会在用户设备本地处理和保存致电者名称、模拟号码、运营商、来电日期时间、用户录制的音频、用户选择的主屏幕截图和铃声，以及铃声、震动、模拟休眠等设置。上述内容由用户主动填写、录制或选择。

二、权限及使用目的

麦克风仅在主动录制致电者声音时使用；照片和铃声通过系统选择器由用户选取，不访问整个相册或文件库。震动用于操作反馈和模拟来电；运动数据用于响铃时判断设备是否被拿起以降低音量；接近检测仅用于应用内通话时的近距离遮屏、防误触。iOS版不识别左右手握持。可拒绝或在系统设置中调整系统允许管理的权限，对应功能可能受影响；震动、接近检测等能力并非都有独立授权开关。表现也取决于设备和iOS版本。

三、处理方式与传感器信息

本应用无需账号，不主动发起业务网络请求，不将模版、录音、图片、铃声或传感器数据发送至运营者服务器。传感器读数仅即时处理，不保存为用户资料。用户选择的云端照片或文件可能由系统及相应服务提供方下载，其处理受用户设置和相应服务政策约束。

四、共享、转让与第三方服务

本应用未接入广告、统计分析、用户画像或第三方数据处理SDK，不会主动向第三方出售、共享或跨境传输应用内模版和媒体。系统备份、云端文件选择及用户主动发送反馈不属于应用自动上传；相关服务按各自规则处理。用户主动通过邮件提供的联系方式、问题描述或附件，将用于回复反馈和排查问题，请勿发送不必要的个人信息。

五、保存期限与删除

模版及私有媒体保存在本机，直至用户修改、删除模版或删除应用；不再使用的私有文件会按应用清理逻辑移除，不删除系统照片或文件中的原件。“卸载App”可能保留文稿与数据；清除本机应用数据应选择“删除App”。本应用未排除系统备份，数据可能随用户启用的iCloud或电脑设备备份保存；删除模版或应用不会自动删除既有备份，请通过Apple提供的设置管理。

六、用户权利

单击模版卡片设定时间，长按修改来电设置，通过删除按钮删除模版及其私有文件。可在iOS设置中管理系统允许调整的权限，通过“删除App”移除本机应用数据，并自行管理设备备份。隐私或反馈信息处理问题可通过本政策邮箱联系运营者。

七、未成年人保护

本应用不以儿童为服务对象，不会主动识别或收集用户年龄。未成年人应根据所在地适用法律，在监护人同意和指导下使用本应用。

八、政策更新

因功能、法律法规或平台规则变化需要更新本政策时，更新后的内容将在应用内展示并标明更新日期；涉及处理方式的重大变化时，将依法提供必要提示。

九、联系我们

如对本政策、个人信息保护或本地数据删除有疑问、意见或投诉，请发送邮件至 l_lsnow@sina.com。

十、隐私网页访问

本网页由GitHub Pages托管。访问网页会连接GitHub的服务器，托管服务可能为提供服务及保障安全处理IP地址、浏览器信息等必要访问技术数据，具体请参阅GitHub隐私声明。本网页未接入额外的广告或访问统计脚本。网页访问与App内的本地情景来电处理是不同场景，不代表App会自动上传模版、录音或其他媒体。

1. Information processed locally

The app locally processes caller details, dates, recordings, screenshots, ringtones and related settings that you enter, record or choose.

2. Permissions and purpose

The microphone is used only when you choose to record a caller voice. System pickers access only the photos and audio files you select, not your entire library. Vibration provides feedback and simulated ringing; motion detects pickup while ringing to lower volume; proximity is used only during an in-app call for near-device screen covering and accidental-touch prevention. iOS does not identify the holding hand. You can deny or change permissions where iOS provides controls; related features may be affected. Vibration and proximity do not each have a separate permission switch. Behavior also depends on the device and iOS version.

3. Processing and sensors

No account is required. The app does not initiate network requests for its call features or send templates, recordings, images, ringtones or sensor readings to the operator’s servers. Sensor readings are processed immediately and not retained as user records. Selected cloud photos or files may be downloaded by the system and their providers, subject to your settings and those services’ policies.

4. Sharing and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, profiling or third-party data-processing SDK. It does not itself sell, share or transfer in-app templates and media abroad. System backups, cloud-file selection and feedback you choose to send are separate from automatic app uploads and follow the relevant services’ rules. Contact details, issue descriptions and attachments you voluntarily email are used to reply and troubleshoot. Please do not send unnecessary personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

Templates and private media remain on the device until you edit or delete a template or delete the app. Unused private files are removed by the app’s cleanup logic; originals in Photos or Files are not deleted. Offload App may retain documents and data; use Delete App to remove local app data. The app does not exclude its data from system backups, so enabled iCloud or computer device backups may include it. Deleting a template or the app does not automatically delete existing backups; manage them using Apple’s settings.

6. Your rights

Tap a template card to set its time, press and hold to edit, and use its delete button to remove the template and private files. Manage permissions where iOS provides controls, use Delete App to remove local app data, and manage device backups separately. Contact the operator at the policy email with privacy or feedback-data questions.

7. Minors

The app is not directed to children and does not identify or collect age. Minors should use it with a guardian's consent and guidance as required by applicable local law.

8. Updates

If features, laws or platform rules change, the revised policy will appear in the app with an updated date. Material changes to processing will receive any notice required by law.

9. Contact

Privacy questions, complaints or deletion requests: l_lsnow@sina.com.

10. Visiting this privacy website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it connects to GitHub’s servers. The hosting service may process necessary technical access data, such as your IP address and browser information, to serve and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page adds no advertising or visitor analytics scripts. Website visits are separate from local simulated-call processing in the app and do not mean the app automatically uploads templates, recordings or other media.
